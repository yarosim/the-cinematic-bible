Most Bible films mix the text and the imagination, and you can’t tell which is which. Our films don’t. Each Part is built on a verse range shown on screen, and each Part ends with a short card called The Ledger.

WRITTEN

The words and events the verses state, quoted word for word from the World English Bible. You can check every line.

IMAGINED

What the verses do not say: faces, clothes, weather, buildings, music. Marked as ours.

Each film is a creative retelling using AI-generated visuals and narrative interpretation based on Scripture. We say so in plain words in every video description and below.
